About Me

Design that

I'm a UI/UX Designer based in Mangalore with a background in Computer Applications. I believe great design is invisible — it just feels right. My work spans the full design cycle: from user research and journey mapping to polished high-fidelity interfaces ready for handoff.

I actively integrate AI tools like Claude AI and Stitch AI into my ideation process, which helps me iterate faster, validate ideas earlier, and create more thoughtful designs. I've collaborated with developer teams and am comfortable working with HTML & CSS to better understand implementation constraints.
